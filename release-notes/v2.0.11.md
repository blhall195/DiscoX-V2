- Shots are quicker. The device used to wait for you to hold it perfectly still before it would take a reading, which could feel fussy with cold hands or on a ledge. Now the laser fires as soon as you press, and the compass and clino are read the whole time the laser is measuring and averaged, so a natural hand-held aim gives a steady reading. If the device moved too much during the shot, the screen says UNSTEADY and you simply take the shot again.
- While plugged in to charge, the battery icon animates and the light shows the charge: red below 80%, orange from 80%, green when full. A shot or the disco lights take over briefly, then the charging colour comes back.
- The device no longer switches itself off for a low battery while it is plugged in, so a flat device you have just put on charge stays on.
- On the newest devices (the ones that switch on by themselves when you plug in the charger), plugging in a switched-off device now shows a charging screen with the battery level to two decimal places. Press any button to use the device, hold the power button to switch it off, or just unplug it. To protect the screen it dims after a minute and turns off after two; any button brings it back.
- Also on the newest devices: a charge limit (Menu → Settings → Charge limit) of 80%, 90% or 100%. Stopping short of full is kinder to the battery if you keep the device on charge between trips. The limit only holds while the device is switched on, so if you switch it off on the charger it charges to full. The default is 100%, which charges exactly as before. And while plugged in, these devices go to the charging screen instead of switching themselves off when left idle.
- Each device now shows its own number on the start-up screen after the firmware version (for example "v2.0.11 #49"). If you contact us about your device, this tells us which one it is.
- New start-up animation: a flight down a cave passage into the light.
- Your settings and calibration are kept when you update, and you don't need to recalibrate.
