- Calibration is a little more accurate. Part 2 of the calibration included a fine-tuning step that turned out to be fitting itself to the small wobbles of a hand-held device rather than to anything real in the sensor. In our test calibration it could push a compass reading off by up to half a degree when the device was steeply tilted or rolled. That step has been removed.
- To get the benefit, run Part 2 of the calibration again (Menu → Calibration). Part 1 does not need repeating. If you don't, nothing changes: your existing calibration carries on working exactly as before.
- The accuracy figure shown at the end of Part 2 may read slightly higher than you are used to. The old figure was flattered by the step that has been removed; the calibration itself is not worse.
- This release keeps the spinning disco ball start-up animation.
- Your settings and calibration are kept when you update.
