- Readings on dark rock work far more often. The laser was refusing many shots on dark or wet surfaces that it could have measured. It now accepts a weaker return signal, but takes three shots for every reading and only keeps the result if all three agree to within 25 mm. A shot that can't be trusted is still refused rather than recorded.
- Each reading takes a little longer, because the laser now takes three shots instead of one.
- This release keeps the spinning disco ball start-up animation.
- No re-calibration is needed. Your settings and calibration are kept when you update.
