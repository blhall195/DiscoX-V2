- You can now look back through your earlier readings on the device. Press DOWN to see your last reading, press DOWN again for the one before, and so on; UP steps forward again, and pressing UP on the newest reading takes you back to the live screen. Each reading shows its distance, compass and clino, and whether it was a leg, a splay or a single shot. The laser switches off while you are looking back, to save battery; press FIRE to carry on surveying. The device keeps your last 500 readings whether or not they reached your phone, so you can check a shot underground even after it has synced. If you have splays switched off, UP also opens your readings.
- DOWN still switches the laser off between stations as before; it now also shows your last reading.
- Your readings are also on the USB drive (Menu → Settings → USB Drive Mode) as HISTORY.CSV, newest first, so you can copy them to a computer.
- New option for drawing plan views on paper: the device can show the horizontal distance of each shot above the slope distance. It is off by default. To switch it on, open CONFIG.JSON in USB drive mode and change show_horizontal from false to true.
- The history starts with this update, so readings taken before it won't appear.
- Your settings and calibration are kept when you update, and you don't need to recalibrate.
